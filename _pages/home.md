I am Leonardo, a physicist working on High Performance Computing (HPC) and Biomolecular systems.

I was born in [Perugia](https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=12/43.1124/12.3885) (you might have heard of it for the chocolate, the [jazz](https://www.umbriajazz.it/) event or [its history](https://www.mapillary.com/app/?lat=43.113783585938&lng=12.388458983136&z=18.814642620856052&menu=true&pKey=163087522407114&focus=photo&x=0.49050182404357917&y=0.40682024111297355&zoom=0)), where I got my bachelor's degree in Physics. I continued my studies in [Padova](https://www.openstreetmap.org/?#map=12/45.3986/11.7878) for a master degree and a Ph.D. in Physics. Currently, I work in [Bologna](https://www.openstreetmap.org/?#map=12/44.4885/11.2285) as HPC software engineer. For more details you can checkout my [curriculum vitae](cv) or my [publication page](publications).
