I am Leonardo Salicari, I am from [Perugia](https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=12/43.1124/12.3885) (you might know that for the chocolate or the [jazz](https://www.umbriajazz.it/)), where I got my my bachelor's degree in Physics. Then, I've moved to [Padua](https://osm.org/go/0IBpoal--?relation=44836) for a master in Physics and to pursuing a Ph.D. in Physics, as well as a carrier as a [medieval fencer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historical_European_martial_arts) (I am working on that). Currently, I live in [Bologna](https://osm.org/go/xdUQufh--?relation=43172). I worked on statistical mechanical models of biopolymers and numerical simulations of these (some say that [I like proteins](https://youtu.be/L2QSMzeSvxk?si=EflSKtiOXzwWEJ2a)). Currently, I am working with supercomputers and High performance computing in the context of Life Science. More info can be found in my [curriculum vitae](cv).

If you are interested in my academic carrier you might want to check out the [publication page](publications).

As a WIP, I collect topics that interested me in the [blog](blog).

I love to play board games, hiking through cool places (from seashores to mountains) and recently took up a [Nikon D3300](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikon_D3300) with an 35 mm (equivalent 52 mm for a full frame) lens and began taking photos of what seems nice. Trying to improve my knowledge about [Fiore dei Liberi's fencing school](https://wiktenauer.com/wiki/Fiore_de%27i_Liberi).
