I am Leonardo, a physicist working on High Performance Computing (HPC) and Biomolecular systems.

I was born in [Perugia](https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=12/43.1124/12.3885) (you might have heard of it for the chocolate, the [jazz](https://www.umbriajazz.it/) event or [its history](https://www.mapillary.com/app/?lat=43.113783585938&lng=12.388458983136&z=18.814642620856052&menu=true&pKey=163087522407114&focus=photo&x=0.49050182404357917&y=0.40682024111297355&zoom=0)), where I got my bachelor's degree in Physics. I continued my studies in [Padova](https://www.openstreetmap.org/?#map=12/45.3986/11.7878) for a master degree and a Ph.D. in Physics. For more details you can checkout my [curriculum vitae](cv) or my [publication page](publications).

This website tries to aggregate info, interests, ideas and everything that I can put on it. I collect these in a [blog](blog) for which an [RSS feed](http://localhost:8080/feed.xml) is available.

Currently, my interests are (in no particular order):
- [late medieval fencing and re-enact](https://wiktenauer.com/wiki/Fiore_de%27i_Liberi)
- Leather crafting
- High performance molecular dynamics simulations
- Self-hosting
- Taking nice looking photos with a second-hand [Nikon D3300](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikon_D3300) and 35mm Nikon lens
